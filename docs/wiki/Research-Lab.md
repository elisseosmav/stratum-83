# Research Lab — Historical Development Note

Earlier STRATUM-83 development versions experimented with batch Minimax-versus-random games and BFS/Dijkstra/A* maze benchmarks.

**The current Games + Algorithms rebuild does not expose Research Lab in its user interface.** It is deliberately focused on human-versus-computer games, and the separate ALGORITHMS view gives short descriptions of the methods used.

Historical research modules and their tests are preserved temporarily in source so prior results are not lost during the UI rebuild. They should not be presented as accessible screens in the new candidate.
